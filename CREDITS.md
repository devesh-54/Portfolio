# Asset & Architecture Credits

## 3D Models & Assets
- **Laptop & Smartphone Low-Poly Base Meshes**:
  - Base geometry source: Poly Pizza & Web3D Open Source Assets collection
  - License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 3.0 / CC0)
